# StudyMate Product Direction

Before changing product flow, UI, feature priority, or AI behavior, read `CONTEXT.md`.

`CONTEXT.md` is the current source of truth. `HACKATHON_CONTEXT.md` and `COPILOT_CONTEXT(new).md` are older planning notes and should not override it.

For the hackathon, StudyMate is one guided workflow: upload material, learn it in Plain Mode or Story Mode, jump into Exam Prep, answer questions, earn XP, and unlock an exam-ready revision PDF.

Avoid expanding the app into many disconnected tools. Existing features like notes, AOC, past questions, quiz, key points, flashcards, and reports should be reused behind this simpler flow.
